Why Sach

Built to keep weather out and comfort in.

Every window in the collection shares the same engineering standards, regardless of style or size.

Triple-seal weatherproofing

A three-channel, equal-pressure sealing design — a patented national utility model — keeps wind-driven rain and drafts out.

6063-T5 aluminum frames

Every profile is powder-coated 6063-T5 aluminum for corrosion resistance, rigidity, and a lasting finish.

Branded German & import hardware

Locking and hinge hardware from Wehag (Germany), Hopo, Caldwell, and other trusted manufacturers, model by model.

Independently tested performance

Water, air, wind-load, sound, and thermal (U-factor) ratings are published for every model — no guesswork when you spec.
